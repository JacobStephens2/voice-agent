# Delete the telephone room where the call actually ended

A Callback Handshake hands its room to a Telephone Session and stops deciding when the call ends, but every handler that hands it over also tore the room down again as it unwound. On the attended Keeplore Button Trial call of 2026-08-26, `handleOutbound` deleted a room the live session had already deleted, and LiveKit answered the second delete with `requested room does not exist` at `apps/worker/src/telephone-agent.ts:558` while the caller heard audio stutter into a disconnect. `handleCompletionOutbound` and `handleCompletionResumption` hand the room over the same way and carried the same unguarded delete.

The room now has one deletion with one home. `roomDeletedOnce` holds the `JobContext` and records the delete that happened rather than the intent to delete, so the live session's `endCall` and the handler's own unwinding are the same call, and the second one is a no-op. Recording the completed delete, not the attempt, is the load-bearing half: a delete the provider refuses leaves the room undeleted, and the handler must still be answerable for it as it unwinds. Setting the flag before the request would turn a refused delete into a leaked room and a call nobody ends.

Who owes the rest of the teardown is a separate question, and it is now one `TeardownOwner` value per handler rather than a spread of booleans. A handshake closes its own fixed speech session and detector as it hands the room over, so `"live-session"` suppresses the second close of one session. A Completion Callback that stands down for a separate recovery job holds the call open for it, so `"recovery"` suppresses the delete alone. Everything else is `"handler"`, which tears down what it still owns.

Reaching this needed a seam. `handleOutbound` is exported and takes an optional `CallbackHandshakeDependencies`, mirroring the injection `runLiveKitTelephoneSession` already accepts, so a test can drive a real Callback Handshake to DTMF authorization and count what the handoff leaves behind without a speech provider, a transcriber, or a real room. The two Completion Callback handlers have no such seam yet and carry the change on the shared helper alone.
