# Require passkey authentication for authority expansion

Registering a codebase or changing its permitted scope or Deployment Contract requires a fresh passkey-authenticated action in the PWA. The Voice Agent may discuss and prepare a proposed change during a Telephone Session, but voice authentication alone cannot expand filesystem, GitHub, service, data, or deployment authority. This adds an intentional visual step only when standing authority changes while preserving unattended work within an already authenticated Standing Autonomy Policy.
