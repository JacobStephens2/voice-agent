# Bound Telephone duration and call evidence

A Telephone Session may last at most two hours and ends earlier after bounded prolonged silence; when only Delegation remains, the Voice Agent offers to disconnect and place a Completion Callback. Callback Handshakes leave no voicemail, while Completion Callbacks may leave only a generic notice directing the user to an authenticated channel. Raw audio and generated speech are not retained. A one-session raw-audio debug exception requires a fresh passkey, is recorded in the Evidence Record, and expires with automatic deletion within 24 hours. Finalized sanitized transcript turns, semantic TTS text, tool events, bounded excerpts, interruption events, and timing metrics are retained for 90 days; interim STT hypotheses are discarded after each turn.
