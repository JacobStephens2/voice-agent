# Retain pending Task Confirmations through unclear speech

A pending Task Confirmation is answered by classifying one sanitized finalized turn as affirmative, negative, or ambiguous. Ordinary affirmative speech confirms, including combined and repeated fragments such as "I confirm—yes", "yes, I confirm", and "that is correct". Explicit negation and qualification win over any affirmative fragment in the same turn, so mixed or conditional speech declines. A bare acknowledgement such as "okay" decides only when it is the whole turn; beside discourse filler it is someone thinking aloud, not consenting. Ambiguous speech confirms nothing and consumes nothing: the same Task Confirmation and its claimed state-changing slot stay pending under ADR 0041, and a later affirmative accepts it without repeating the readback.

A Bounded Provider Retry is spent at most once per Telephone Session. The first transient coordinator or text-to-speech failure — and, under ADR-0052, the first fault the Voice Agent raises in its own turn handling — replays the work under the durable turn identity it already had, and the first transient recognition failure, which cannot be replayed, asks the caller to repeat into the same pending state. A fault with nothing left to replay — an unusable session, a failed start, or a turn already answered — is terminal on its first report. Any remaining failure records the failing component, plays the prerecorded response exactly once, and ends the call under ADR 0044 while preserving every accepted Durable Task for recovery.
